This interdisciplinary workshop focuses on water, soil, and air-related hazards, providing practical exposure to tools such as GC–MS, ICP–MS, CHNS analysis, GIS-based landslide prediction, and atmospheric modeling.

  • Hands-on water quality assessment (pH, EC, etc.)
  • Landslide prediction using ArcGIS
  • Atmospheric modeling using WRF & GrADS

Day 2

  • Guest lecture by Dr. Reet Kamal Tiwari (IIT Ropar)
  • From Pixels to Protection: Tracking Natural Hazards from Space
  • Satellite & geospatial tools discussion
  • Interactive session + concluding insights
