Material Science Workshop

Explore the fundamental techniques used to study materials, from light–matter interactions to real-world characterization methods.

This workshop offers a window into the foundational methods used to study the materials around us. By blending technical talks with live demonstrations, we aim to explore the ubiquitous tools of the field and the specific flavour of how light interacts with matter.
