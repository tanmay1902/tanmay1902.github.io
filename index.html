01 / About me

Membranes, under the lens.

I'm a BS–MS student at IISER Mohali, working on membrane mechanics, giant plasma membrane vesicles and quantitative image analysis.

My bench is part wet-lab, part code: I isolate GPMVs from living cells, image them for hours, and build the pipelines that turn every frame into tension, rigidity and shape.

Right now: mapping how membrane blebs change mechanically as cells go through regulated necrotic death — at the Max Planck Institute of Biophysics.
